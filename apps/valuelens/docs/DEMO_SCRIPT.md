# ValueLens Demo Script

## 15 second opener

Most companies are playing with AI, but very few can prove which AI projects are actually worth funding. ValueLens fixes that.

## 60 second demo

1. Start on the ValueLens landing screen.
2. Explain the loop: Input, forecast, risk, build, ledger, learn.
3. Select IdeaLens, ProcessLens, or CodeLens.
4. Enter a real business problem.
5. Generate the report.
6. Point to four numbers: forecast annual value, risk level, confidence, and payback.
7. Show the recommended first build.
8. Show the 30 / 60 / 90 roadmap.
9. End on the outcome ledger.

## Closing line

ValueLens is the missing governance layer between AI hype and funded AI execution.

## Judge-facing explanation

The important part is not only the forecast. The important part is the loop. ValueLens keeps forecast value and actual value separate, then forces the team to review the gap after deployment. That means the tool does not just generate confidence; it creates accountability.

## Safe CodeLens wording

CodeLens is a shadow audit. It analyses the structure and readiness of a codebase, then recommends safe next actions. It does not modify production code.
