# ValueLens Judge Scorecard

## What the judges should understand in 30 seconds

ValueLens answers the question most AI demos avoid:

Is this AI project worth funding, and how will we prove it worked?

## Judging strengths

### Valuable AI

ValueLens links AI ideas to estimated annual value, payback, confidence, and measurable outcomes.

### Practical impact

The tool is useful for founders, SMEs, consultants, charities, and product teams. It helps them avoid funding the wrong AI project.

### Governance

Forecast value and actual value are separated. Every project must return to the outcome ledger after launch.

### Risk control

High-risk projects default to pilot mode and human approval. CodeLens is shadow-audit only.

### Demo clarity

The whole story can be shown in one minute:

1. Enter problem
2. Generate value case
3. Review risk
4. Show roadmap
5. End on outcome ledger

## Differentiator

Most AI hackathon projects are point solutions. ValueLens is a meta-layer that helps decide which AI projects deserve to exist.

## Killer line

ValueLens stops teams funding AI theatre and starts funding AI that pays back.
