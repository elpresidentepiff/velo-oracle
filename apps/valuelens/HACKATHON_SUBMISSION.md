# ValueLens Hackathon Submission

## Product

ValueLens

## One-line pitch

ValueLens turns an AI idea, business process, or codebase into a board-ready value case, risk audit, implementation roadmap, and outcome ledger.

## Problem

Organisations are flooded with AI ideas, but most teams cannot tell which projects are worth funding, what the delivery risk is, or whether the promised value was ever achieved after launch.

## Solution

ValueLens gives teams a fast, structured way to assess AI opportunities before they spend money. It estimates annual value, payback period, risk, confidence, recommended first build, and success metrics. It then creates an outcome ledger so forecast value can be compared against actual value after deployment.

## What makes it different

Most AI tools generate answers. ValueLens records the forecast, audits the result, and learns from the gap.

## MVP features

- IdeaLens for AI project ideas
- ProcessLens for business workflow analysis
- CodeLens for codebase readiness and AI integration opportunities
- ROI/value forecast
- Risk score and confidence score
- 30 / 60 / 90 day implementation roadmap
- Board-ready verdict
- Outcome ledger placeholder

## CodeLens boundary

CodeLens does not modify production code. It produces a shadow audit first, flags obvious risks, and recommends safe next steps. High-risk automations default to human approval.

## Target users

- Founders
- SMEs
- Operations leaders
- Consultants
- Finance teams
- Charities and social impact organisations
- Product teams deciding which AI project to build first

## Demo script

1. Open the MVP.
2. Select IdeaLens, ProcessLens, or CodeLens.
3. Enter a project or codebase summary.
4. Adjust people affected, hours wasted, hourly cost, build complexity, data sensitivity, and evidence quality.
5. Generate the ValueLens report.
6. Show the forecast annual value, risk level, confidence, payback period, recommended first build, roadmap, success metrics, and outcome ledger.

## Closing line

ValueLens is the missing governance layer between AI hype and funded AI execution.
